Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 24 November 2026

IVELRO wants ivelro.com to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities, older people, and people using phones, screen readers or a keyboard. This statement explains what we do, where we stand and how to tell us about problems.

1. Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the standard referred to by the European accessibility rules.

2. Conformance status

We believe ivelro.com is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA: most of the website meets the standard, but some parts may not yet fully meet it. This is based on our own testing; the website has not yet been audited by an external organisation.

3. What we do

  • Clear structure with headings and landmarks, so screen readers can navigate the pages;
  • every function can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator;
  • text and background colours with readable contrast;
  • text alternatives for meaningful images;
  • animations are reduced or turned off when your device asks for reduced motion;
  • pages adapt to screen sizes from small phones to large screens, and text can be enlarged without breaking the layout;
  • separate English and Greek pages with the correct language set, so assistive technologies read them correctly;
  • forms with visible labels and clear error messages.

We test every page on screen widths from 360 to 1440 pixels and check keyboard use before publishing.

4. Known limitations

  • Some decorative animations on the home page may still be noticeable for some visitors; they stop when reduced motion is turned on.
  • Our sample websites are demonstrations and may not meet every requirement in the same way as our main pages.
  • Content provided by third parties, such as fonts or embedded services, is outside our full control.

We work to fix known issues and to improve accessibility with every update.

5. Websites we build for clients

We follow the same accessibility practices in the websites we build for our clients. If a client needs a specific level of accessibility, for example because of legal requirements, we agree on it in the proposal.

6. Feedback and contact

If you find a barrier on our website, or need information in a different format, please tell us at info@ivelro.com. Please tell us the page and the problem. We will reply within 5 working days and try to fix the problem or offer an alternative.

7. About this statement

This statement was prepared on 24 November 2026, based on a self-assessment by the IVELRO team. We will review it at least once a year and whenever the website changes significantly.
