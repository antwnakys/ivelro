## Project rules

- The ivelro.com design (round 2) is final. Do not redesign `index.html`, `el/index.html` or `assets/css/style.css`; small additions only.
- English and Greek are separate pages (`/` and `/el/`). Any text change on one goes on the other too.
- `staging/` is not published. `staging/enable.py` switches on the contact section and analytics.
- `templates/` and `docs/` are internal (not published). Client sites are made with `templates/new-site.py`.
- Industry pages (`websites/`, `el/istoselides/`) and guides (`el/odigoi/`) are generated by `tools/build_pages.py`. Edit the texts there and rerun it; do not edit the generated HTML by hand. New guides go in its `GUIDES` list.
- "Contact us" buttons carry `data-contact-cta hidden` until `staging/enable.py` runs.
- Deploy: push to `main`; `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes only the folders listed in its `cp` line.
- Test with Playwright at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px: no sideways scrolling, no console errors.
- `hq-f900bfutnxls/` is the private team dashboard (clients, progress, domains). Never link it, never add it to the sitemap, never mention its address in the README. Its data lives in the private Google Sheet "Client websites tracker"; the back end is `tools/dashboard/Code.gs` (Apps Script, password checked there). Put the web app URL in `var API` in its `app.js`.
- Policies (privacy, cookies, terms of use, terms of service, payments and refunds, support, accessibility) live at `legal/` and `el/nomika/`, generated by `tools/build_legal.py` from `tools/legal/en/*.html` and `tools/legal/el/*.html`. Edit the texts there (both languages) and rerun it; the Google Docs copies are in Drive → IVELRO → Policies (Web Developer) and must be updated too (`--docs OUTDIR` writes upload-ready files). Do not mention AI in the policies except the AI automation service.
