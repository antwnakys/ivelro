This Privacy Policy explains what personal data IVELRO collects, why we collect it, how we protect it, who we share it with and what rights you have. It applies to our website ivelro.com, to messages you send us, and to the personal data we handle when we work with clients. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, “GDPR”) and Greek Law 4624/2019.

1. Who we are

IVELRO (“IVELRO”, “we”, “us”) is a web development and business automation team based in Athens, Greece. IVELRO is not yet a registered company. Until it is, IVELRO is run by its founding members, and the member named in each agreement acts on behalf of the team. For the purposes of the GDPR, IVELRO is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for anything related to privacy: info@ivelro.com. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, because the law does not require it for our activities; the address above reaches the team member responsible for privacy.

2. The data we collect and why

2.1 When you visit our website

When you open a page on ivelro.com, your browser automatically sends technical information to the servers that deliver the website: your IP address, the date and time, the page you requested, the referring page, and your browser and device type. Our hosting provider uses this information to deliver the page, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and may keep it in server logs for a short period.

  • Purpose: to show you the website and keep it secure.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

ivelro.com does not use advertising, does not build profiles of visitors and does not set cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2.2 Fonts

Our pages use fonts provided by Google Fonts. To display them, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and the page address. Google states that the Google Fonts service does not set cookies and does not use this information to build profiles.

  • Purpose: to display the website consistently on every device.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a consistent, readable design (Article 6(1)(f)).

2.3 Visitor statistics

To understand how many people visit the website and which pages are useful, we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies or similar storage, does not track you across websites, and shows us only totals such as page views, countries and device types. We do not combine it with any other data.

  • Purpose: to improve the website.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used (Article 6(1)(f)).

2.4 When you contact us

When you write to us by email or through a contact form on our website, we receive the details you give us: usually your name, email address, phone number (if you include it), business name and your message. Contact forms on our website are delivered to our inbox by the form service Web3Forms.

  • Purpose: to answer your message and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal.
  • Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f)).

2.5 When you become a client

When we work together, we process the details needed to run the project and the relationship: names and contact details of the people we work with, billing details, the agreement and proposal, project communications, files you send us (such as texts, photos and logos), and access details for accounts you ask us to set up or manage.

  • Purpose: to deliver the services, support you, invoice and keep proper records.
  • Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)) and compliance with tax and accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Passwords and access details are stored only in an encrypted password manager shared by the team, never in emails, documents or chat.

2.6 Business contacts and prospects

To find businesses that could benefit from our services, we may collect business contact details that the business itself has made public, such as the business name, its public phone number, public email address, website and address. We may contact the business once to introduce our services.

  • Purpose: to introduce our services to businesses.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in promoting our services to businesses (Article 6(1)(f)).

You can object at any time by replying “no” or writing to info@ivelro.com. We will then stop contacting you and delete your details, keeping only what is needed to make sure we do not contact you again.

2.7 Data we process on behalf of our clients

The websites, forms and automations we build for clients often collect data from the client’s own customers, for example booking requests or sign-ups. For that data, the client is the controller and IVELRO acts as a processor: we process it only on the client’s documented instructions, to build, test, maintain and support the service. The client’s own privacy policy explains how it uses that data. If you are a customer of one of our clients, please contact that business directly; we will help them answer your request.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run IVELRO, and only as far as each one needs it:

  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): domain name service, email routing and storage for our inboxes, and visitor statistics.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): fonts, and file storage and spreadsheets for our internal documents and client records.
  • Web3Forms: delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Resend (USA): delivers the emails we send.
  • Apple (iCloud Passwords): end-to-end encrypted storage of the team’s access details.

We may also share data with professional advisers (such as an accountant or lawyer) who are bound by confidentiality, and with public authorities when the law requires it. If IVELRO becomes a registered company, the data will pass to that company, which will continue to protect it under this policy.

4. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of our providers are based in the United States or may process data there. When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, together with additional safeguards where needed.

5. How long we keep data

  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security, as set by the provider.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 2 years after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client records: for as long as we work together and afterwards for as long as Greek tax and accounting law requires (usually 5 years after the end of the relevant tax year), or longer only if needed to establish or defend legal claims.
  • Business contacts and prospects: until you object, or at most 2 years after our last contact.
  • Data processed for clients: deleted or returned to the client when our work for them ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. How we protect data

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website and services, limit access to personal data to the team members who need it, keep access details in an encrypted password manager, turn on two-step verification on our important accounts, and choose providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you and the authorities about a data breach when the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • Rectification of data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Erasure of your data, when there is no longer a reason to keep it;
  • Restriction of processing in certain situations;
  • Data portability: to receive data you gave us in a common format, or have it sent to someone else;
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including at any time to direct marketing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent, without affecting what happened before.

To exercise any right, write to info@ivelro.com. We will answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. Exercising your rights is free.

You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), Kifisias Avenue 1-3, 115 23 Athens, www.dpa.gr. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

8. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 15. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

10. Links to other websites

Our website links to other websites, including our sample websites and third-party services. Their owners are responsible for how they handle your data. Our sample websites are demonstrations: their forms do not send your details anywhere.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will make it clear on this page and, where appropriate, tell our clients directly.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: info@ivelro.com, IVELRO, Athens, Greece.
