This policy explains how pricing, invoicing, payments, cancellations and refunds work for IVELRO projects and support packages. It forms part of our Terms of Service. Where a signed agreement says something different, the agreement applies.

1. Prices

  • Every project has a fixed price stated in the written proposal, in euros (€). We do not start work without an agreed price.
  • Prices include all the work described in the proposal. Extra work is priced and agreed in writing before we start it.
  • Where VAT or other taxes apply, they are shown separately in the proposal.
  • Third-party costs, such as software or AI model subscriptions, hardware for local AI, paid tools, SMS credits and payment fees, are not included unless the proposal says so. They are normally paid by you directly to the provider.

2. Payment schedule for projects

Unless the proposal sets a different schedule:

PaymentAmountWhen
First payment50% of the project priceWhen you accept the proposal. Work starts once it is received.
Final payment50% of the project priceOn delivery, before the final hand-over of access details and the switch to live use.

For larger projects, the proposal may split the price into more stages, each linked to a milestone.

3. Support packages

  • The first month of support after delivery is free.
  • After that, if you choose a support package, its monthly fee is invoiced at the start of each month.
  • You can stop a support package at any time with 30 days’ written notice. It ends at the end of the notice period.
  • The contents of each package are described in our Support and Maintenance Policy.

4. Invoices and how to pay

  • Payments are due within 7 days of the request for payment, unless the proposal says otherwise.
  • Payments are made by bank transfer to the account stated in the agreement or the request for payment. Please include the reference we give you.
  • IVELRO will never ask you to pay to a different account by phone or chat. If you receive such a request, please check with us first at info@ivelro.com.

5. Late payment

If a payment is late, we send a friendly reminder. If it is still unpaid 14 days after the reminder, we may pause the project or support until it is paid, and the timeline moves accordingly. We hand over full access to a finished project only after it has been paid in full.

6. Cancelling a project

  • Before work starts: if you cancel before we have started working, we refund the first payment in full.
  • After work starts: you pay for the work done up to the date of cancellation. The first payment is not refundable once work has started. If the work done is worth more than the first payment, we invoice the difference; you receive the work done up to that date once it is paid.
  • If we cancel: if IVELRO ends a project for reasons that are not your fault, we refund any amount you have paid for work we have not delivered.
  • Paused projects: if a project is paused for more than 30 days because we are waiting for you, we may treat it as cancelled and invoice the work done.

7. Refunds

  • Because our work is custom-made for each client, completed and accepted work is not refundable.
  • If something does not work as agreed, we fix it free of charge during the 30-day warranty period described in our Terms of Service. We will always try to fix a problem before discussing a refund.
  • Monthly support fees already invoiced for the current month are not refundable.
  • Approved refunds are paid by bank transfer within 14 days.

8. Right of withdrawal for consumers

Our services are intended for businesses. If you are a consumer and you agreed to the project at a distance (for example by email), you have the right to withdraw within 14 days of the agreement without giving a reason, under Greek Law 2251/1994. If you asked us in writing to start work during those 14 days and then withdraw, you pay for the work done up to that point. If the work is fully completed during those 14 days at your express request, and you confirmed that you would then lose the right of withdrawal, that right no longer applies. To withdraw, send us a clear statement by email.

9. Questions and disputes

If you have a question or disagree with an invoice, please write to info@ivelro.com within 14 days of receiving it. We will reply within 5 working days and work with you to resolve it.
