Getting here

Nafplio is two hours from Athens by car or bus. Alkyoni is in the pedestrian old town.

By carAthens–Corinth–Argos highway, 145 km. Park at the harbour car park, 4 minutes on foot.
By busKTEL buses every hour from Athens (Kifissos station). The Nafplio bus station is 6 minutes away.
From the airportWe can arrange a transfer from Athens airport. Ask us when you book.
