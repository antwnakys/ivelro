This policy explains how IVELRO supports websites and automations after delivery: what is included for free, what our support packages cover, how to request help and how quickly we respond. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Free fixes after delivery

For 30 days after delivery, we fix free of charge anything in the deliverables that does not work as agreed. The first month of support is also free, so you can see how a support package works before deciding.

2. Support packages

After the first month, you can choose a monthly support package. The price of each package is stated in your proposal.

3. How to request support

  • Send every request by email to support@ivelro.com, so nothing gets lost. If you call or message a team member, they will forward it there.
  • Describe what you need, the page it concerns, and include screenshots, new texts or photos where possible.
  • Support hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 19:00 (Athens time), except Greek public holidays.

5. How we handle a request

  1. We log the request and confirm that we received it.
  2. We assign it to the team member who built the project.
  3. If the request is not covered for free or by your package, we send a price first and wait for your approval.
  4. Another team member checks the result before we tell you it is done.
  5. We let you know and close the request.

6. Monthly check

For clients with a support package, once a month we check that the website opens correctly on phones and computers, that HTTPS works, that forms deliver messages, that automations have run, and when the domain is due for renewal. We let you know if everything is working, or what we fixed.

7. What support does not cover

  • Problems caused by changes made by you or others to the website, accounts or settings;
  • outages, changes or price increases of third-party services, although we will help you deal with them;
  • the content you publish, and its legal correctness;
  • training, new features or redesigns, which are quoted separately.

8. Domains and renewals

Domains are registered in your name. With a support package, we monitor renewal dates and remind you in good time. Renewing and paying for the domain remains your responsibility unless agreed otherwise; if a domain expires, the website and any related email stop working.

9. Ending support

You can stop a support package at any time with 30 days’ written notice. When support ends, we hand over any access details you need and remove our access to your accounts when you ask us to. See our Payments and Refunds Policy for billing.
