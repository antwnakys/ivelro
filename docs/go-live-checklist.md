# Go-live checklist

Go through this before a system goes live for a client.

## Automations and integrations
- [ ] Every workflow tested end to end with the client's real tools and sample data
- [ ] Errors are caught and someone is notified (automation@ivelro.com and the client contact)
- [ ] AI steps produce drafts or suggestions that a person reviews where it matters
- [ ] No passwords or keys in code, documents or chat; stored in Apple Passwords
- [ ] Accounts and subscriptions are in the client's name; renewal dates in the dashboard

## Local AI systems
- [ ] Runs on the agreed hardware; restarts cleanly after a power cut
- [ ] Only approved company documents are loaded; access by role or department works
- [ ] Answers to a list of known questions are correct and show their sources
- [ ] Nothing leaves the client's systems unless agreed in writing
- [ ] Backup and update plan agreed and written in the handover document

## AI search presence
- [ ] Company facts (name, services, area, hours, contact, prices if public) are identical everywhere
- [ ] Structured company information is published and valid
- [ ] Baseline recorded: how AI assistants describe the company before the work
- [ ] Client approved every public text
- [ ] No promises of rankings or guaranteed results in any client material

## Handover
- [ ] Team trained; short user guide delivered
- [ ] Client has the logins they need; IVELRO access listed and removable
- [ ] Support package and first monthly check date agreed
