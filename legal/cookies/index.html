Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains whether and how ivelro.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, session storage and tracking pixels, work in a comparable way. They can be used to make a website work, to remember your choices, to measure visits, or to track you for advertising.

Under the EU ePrivacy rules and Greek Law 3471/2006, a website may store or read information on your device without your consent only when this is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Anything else needs your consent first.

2. Our short answer

ivelro.com does not set cookies, does not use advertising or tracking technologies, and does not use social media pixels. Because we use no cookies that need consent, you will not see a cookie banner on our website.

4. Private team area

Our website includes a private, password-protected area used only by IVELRO team members. When a team member signs in, the browser’s session storage keeps the sign-in for up to 6 hours, and it is deleted when the browser tab is closed. This is strictly necessary for the area to work and is never used for visitors.

5. Websites we link to

When you follow a link to another website, that website may use its own cookies under its own policy. We do not control them.

6. How to control cookies

You can see, block and delete cookies in your browser settings. Help pages for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking cookies does not affect ivelro.com, because it does not rely on them.

7. Changes

If we ever start using cookies or similar technologies that need consent, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting them. The date at the top shows the latest version.
