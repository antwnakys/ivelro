This policy explains how IVELRO supports the systems we deliver, such as automations, integrations, local AI systems and AI search presence work: what is included for free, what our support packages cover, how to request help and how quickly we respond. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Free fixes after delivery

For 30 days after delivery, we fix free of charge anything in the deliverables that does not work as agreed. The first month of support is also free, so you can see how a support package works before deciding.

3. How to request support

  • Send every request by email to support@ivelro.com, so nothing gets lost. If you call or message a team member, they will forward it there.
  • Describe what you need and which system it concerns, and include screenshots, examples or new texts where possible.
  • Support hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 19:00 (Athens time), except Greek public holidays.

5. How we handle a request

  1. We log the request and confirm that we received it.
  2. We assign it to the team member responsible for that system.
  3. If the request is not covered for free or by your package, we send a price first and wait for your approval.
  4. Another team member checks the result before we tell you it is done.
  5. We let you know and close the request.

6. Monthly check

For clients with a support package, once a month we check that automations have run without errors, that integrations still exchange data correctly, that local AI systems respond and use up-to-date company documents, and that the accounts and subscriptions your systems depend on are active. For AI search presence clients on Plus, we also check how AI search tools describe your business. We let you know if everything is working, or what we fixed.

7. What support does not cover

  • Problems caused by changes made by you or others to the systems, accounts or settings;
  • outages, changes or price increases of third-party services, although we will help you deal with them;
  • hardware faults, unless agreed otherwise;
  • the content you publish or give us, and its legal correctness;
  • training of new staff, new features or new systems, which are quoted separately.

8. Third-party accounts and costs

Accounts and subscriptions your systems rely on, such as automation platforms, AI model providers or software licences, are in your name. Paying for them remains your responsibility unless agreed otherwise; if a subscription lapses, the related automation or AI feature may stop working. With a support package, we remind you in good time about renewals we know of.

9. Ending support

You can stop a support package at any time with 30 days’ written notice. When support ends, we hand over any access details and documentation you need and remove our access to your systems and accounts when you ask us to. See our Payments and Refunds Policy for billing.
