# How an IVELRO project runs

Internal guide. The stages match the client dashboard: Signed → Discovery → Build → Testing → Live (or Paused).

## Roles

| Role | Person | In a project |
|---|---|---|
| Sales & Partnerships | Iasonas | First contact, discovery call, proposal, agreement, invoices, main client contact |
| AI Search & Content Lead | Konstantinos | AI search audit, company facts and content, consistency across sources, monthly AI answer checks |
| Brand & Communication Designer | Haris | Proposal and report layout, diagrams of the client's system, training material, IVELRO visuals |
| Developer | Antonis | Builds the AI-enabled systems, automations, integrations and local AI deployments; technical implementation of AI search presence (structured data, machine-readable company information) |
| AI Solutions Specialist | George | Maps the client's workflows, designs the solution with the Developer, configures and tests, trains staff, owns support after delivery |

## 1. Signed (Sales & Partnerships)
- Agreement accepted and first payment received. Client added to the dashboard with service, responsible person and next step.
- Send the "What we need from you" checklist.

## 2. Discovery (AI Solutions Specialist, with the Developer)
- Map how the work is done today: steps, tools, who does what, where time is lost.
- For AI search presence: the AI Search & Content Lead records how AI assistants and AI search tools currently describe the company.
- For local AI: agree data sources, number of users, access rules, privacy requirements and the hardware it will run on.
- Write a one-page solution outline; the client approves it.

## 3. Build (Developer)
- Automations and integrations: connect the client's tools, build the workflows, add AI steps where they help, with a person reviewing important outputs.
- Local AI: install and configure the system on the agreed hardware, load the approved company documents, set access by role.
- AI search presence: publish clear, consistent company information and structured data; correct sources the Content Lead identified.
- All accounts and subscriptions are in the client's name. Access details go in the IVELRO group in Apple Passwords, never in email or chat.

## 4. Testing (AI Solutions Specialist)
- Test with real tools and sample data; test local AI answers against known questions and check sources are cited.
- Check privacy: who can see what, where data is stored, that nothing leaves the client's systems unless agreed.
- Go through docs/go-live-checklist.md. The client tests and approves in writing.

## 5. Live
- Switch to live use, train the team, hand over documentation.
- Dashboard: stage Live, launch date, support package, next renewal.
- Sales sends the final invoice; ask for a short testimonial.
- Clients on a support package get the monthly check.
