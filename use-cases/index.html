01 · AI Search Presence

Be described accurately when customers ask AI.

More and more customers ask AI assistants and AI-powered search for recommendations. We make sure the information those tools find about your business is clear, consistent and trustworthy. We do not promise rankings, because no one controls how AI tools choose their answers. We make your business easy to understand and easy to recommend.

Accurate answers

AI tools describe what you do, where you work and how to reach you correctly.

Consistent information

Services, opening hours, prices and contact details match wherever they appear.

Clear structure

Your company information is published in a structured form that machines read reliably.

Credibility

Facts, credentials and reviews about your business are easy to verify.

Regular checks

We check what AI tools say about you and correct what is wrong or missing.

Plain reporting

A short report on what changed, written in business language.

  • 01
    A dental practice

    AI assistants still mention old opening hours and a branch that has closed. We correct the sources, publish clear service and location details, and check the answers again a month later.

  • 02
    A software company

    AI tools confuse it with a company with a similar name. We clarify its company facts, products and team across its own channels and trusted directories.

  • 03
    A guesthouse

    Travellers asking AI for quiet places to stay by the sea get an outdated description. We rewrite its key facts and amenities in clear, consistent language.

Less repetitive work, fewer manual steps.

Most businesses lose hours every week to copying data, sending the same messages and chasing reminders. We automate that work and connect the tools you already use, then bring AI into the steps where it genuinely helps, with your people staying in control.

Requests handled automatically

Enquiries, bookings and sign-ups are recorded, confirmed and passed to the right person.

Connected tools

Email, calendars, spreadsheets, accounting and CRM share the same information.

Reminders and follow-ups

Payments, appointments and renewals are followed up on time, automatically.

Reports without copy-paste

Weekly and monthly figures are collected and sent without manual work.

AI where it helps

Draft replies, summaries and sorted messages, always reviewed by your team.

Fewer errors

Less retyping means fewer mistakes and a clearer picture of the business.

  • 01
    A tutoring centre

    Parents sign up online; each sign-up goes straight into a spreadsheet, the family gets a confirmation and payment reminders go out on their own.

  • 02
    An accounting office

    Clients receive automatic reminders to send their documents, and every file is named and stored in the right folder.

  • 03
    A sales team

    AI drafts replies to common enquiries from the company’s own answers, and a salesperson checks and sends them.

Answers from your own documents

Faster onboarding

Routine work automated

First drafts, summaries and standard documents prepared in seconds.

Private by design

Sized to you

From one capable office computer for a small team to servers for a large company.

Connected to your tools

Works with the file storage, email and systems you already use.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee that AI tools will recommend us?

No. No one can guarantee how AI tools choose or rank their answers. We make sure your information is accurate, consistent and trustworthy, which gives AI tools what they need to describe and recommend you correctly.

Is AI search presence the same as SEO?

No. We do not sell traditional search engine optimisation. Our work focuses on how AI assistants and answer tools understand your company: clear facts, consistent information and signs of trust.

Do we need new hardware for local AI?

Not always. A small team can often start with one capable computer, and larger companies may use their own servers. We recommend a setup after looking at your needs.

Which tools can you connect?

Most tools that offer an API or export: email, calendars, spreadsheets, CRM, accounting, forms and messaging.

How long does a project take?

A focused automation usually takes days to a few weeks; a local AI system a few weeks, depending on its size. The timeline is in the proposal.

Tell us what slows your business down.

The first conversation is free. We will tell you honestly what is worth doing.
